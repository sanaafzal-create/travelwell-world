## WHY THIS IS THE FOUNDATION
We're not booking a trip — we're building a **Travel ID for life.** The output is the traveler's **Identity Card**, their permanent anchor. And per the Lifetime Loop, *TravelWell wins by capturing the traveler's lifetime, not a booking.* Get this right and every future trip starts from a real picture of them.

**And this is where our motto is earned — *"If It's Safer Informed Travel… TravelWell."*** The capabilities, limitations, and safety facts we gather here are what make the trip genuinely *safer* and *informed* — and we use them to build the trip *around* the traveler, never to limit them.

## THE SIX-STEP IDENTITY BUILDER

### Step 1 — WHO ARE YOU? (name + age range)
**Age shapes pace, tone, mobility, and itinerary — NEVER budget.** (Separate axis; canon rule.) Each range carries a real energy/rest picture:

**Children & teens**
- **Infant & Toddler (0–3)** — Energy: none of their own (stroller/carrier). Day runs on nap-and-feed. Constant rest.
- **Young Child (4–8)** — Energy: good in bursts (≈5,000–10,000 steps). A few active hours, then a break; naps at the young end.
- **Tween (9–12)** — Energy: high (≈10,000–18,000 steps). Keeps up with adults; minimal rest.
- **Teen (13–17)** — Energy: **very high, full grown-up** (≈12,000–20,000+ steps). Dawn to dark, no rest needed. Adult-capable across activities.

**Adults**
- **Young Adult (18–24)** — Energy: peak of a lifetime (≈15,000–25,000+ steps). All day and night.
- **Early Adult (25–34)** — Energy: very high (≈12,000–18,000). Full days easy. The honeymoon/couples core.
- **Established Adult (35–44)** — Energy: high (≈10,000–15,000). Full days, but the first "back, knees" awareness.
- **Peak Earner (45–54)** — Energy: high but managed (≈8,000–14,000). Rest days now *planned*; heat + early starts matter.

**Seniors (highest-spend end — rest matters most)**
- **Pre-Retirement (55–64)** — Energy: steady/moderate (≈6,000–12,000). A good day out; likes an easier pace.
- **Young Senior (65–74)** — Energy: moderate (≈4,000–8,000). A good morning outing, then ease off.
- **Senior (75–84)** — Energy: low/gentle (≈2,000–5,000). A few active hours, then real rest; short walks, frequent sit-downs.
- **Senior+ (85+)** — Energy: very low (under ≈2,000). A "rest-and-visit" rhythm — one outing, then rest; everything around comfort + safety.

**Then the overlay that beats the number — the "Safer Informed" ask:** Atlas asks, warmly, *what physical limitations or safety issues should we know about — and what are you fully able to do?* Both sides: capabilities AND limitations, pace, rest/naps, accessibility, any medical factor (e.g. a heart condition). **The promise, always: we use every fact to build the trip AROUND you — never to hand you limitations.** Enabling, not restricting. (A 35-year-old with a heart condition = a 65-year-old with one; the answer overrides the age default.) This ask is exactly why we get to say **"If It's Safer Informed Travel… TravelWell."**

### Step 2 — HOW DO YOU LIKE TO MOVE? (activity + access)
- **Activity level:** Very Active · Moderately Active · Lightly Active · Leisurely Strolls.
- **Access needs:** Wheelchair · Cane · Frequent Rest · No Stairs · Some Stairs OK · Fully Mobile.
Sets the **shape and tempo** of the itinerary.

### Step 3 — WHAT'S YOUR BUDGET STYLE? (Well-by-Well + "Mix my ranges")
Budget is **per Well, not one tier for the whole trip.** Five ranges: **Essential · Comfort · Premier · Luxury · Ultra.**
The most human option (canon): **"Mix my ranges"** — pick **up to three** to float between per Well; we show options across them; go higher or lower, just tell Atlas.
*Real life: a nicer room (Premier), street-market some nights (Essential), splurge dinners others (Luxury).*
**Exception — Fly-Well** isn't the five tiers; it's **cabin classes: First · Business · Premium Economy · Economy** (3–4 by aircraft).

### Step 4 — WHO'S TRAVELING WITH YOU? (party)
Adults · children (with ages) · seniors (with ages). Activates Family / Senior / Group / Premium.

### Step 5 — WHAT KIND OF TRIP? (interests)
Pick interests or **"Mix my interests"** → *"Here are the Worlds that match you best."*

### Step 6 — TELL US IN YOUR OWN WORDS (the vision)
The dream in **a couple of sentences** (one's too short). Interpreted into Worlds · activities · budget · mobility · timing. It **changes every trip** — beach this year, winter ski next — and that's right: the ID holds the **constant** (who they are); the vision is the **variable** (what they want this time).

## THE OUTPUT — THE IDENTITY CARD
Pace · access needs · budget style (per Well) · trip type · preferred Worlds · interpreted vision · recommended destinations/activities/gear · itinerary style · seasonal timing. **The permanent anchor. The foundation of the OS.**

## THE LIVING ID — THE LIFETIME TRAVELER LOOP
**Attract → Guide → Extend → Reactivate → Repeat.** Built once, **refreshed every trip.** The next time they open TravelWell — maybe a year later — Atlas picks up right where the ID left off and does a short, warm check-in:
- **Age** — a year on; cohort may shift.
- **Health** — better, worse, or the same? *"Still feeling up to what you did last trip?"*
- **Budget** — moved up, down, or held?
- **Interests** — grown, changed, or the same?
- **This trip's vision** — what are they picturing now?
We **never make them start over** — confirm, refresh, and the options we show always look like what they just described.
